AllocStats

Engine/Memory/AllocStats.h final namespace hbe One registered allocator's profiling record: identity, capacity, usage peaks and traffic counters, owned and updated by the MemoryManager under its stats lock

Class description

class AllocStats final at Engine/Memory/AllocStats.h is one allocator's profiling record: what it was registered as, how much it was given, how much it holds, and the peaks and counters that describe the traffic it has served. The engine keeps one per registered allocator, embedded in that allocator's AllocatorProxy record under PROFILE_ENABLED (Engine/Memory/MemoryManager.h), and the class compiles in every configuration — with PROFILE_ENABLED 0, the setting in Engine/Config/BuildConfig.h, only Report's body disappears and every other member still exists.

Its whole life is owned by the MemoryManager, which is the only writer the module shows. RegisterAllocator fills a record through OnRegister; the reporting paths (ReportAllocation, ReportDeallocation) move the counters under the manager's statsLock; DeregisterAllocator refuses release while usage > 0, warns of a leak, and on the clean path calls Reset before the record returns to the free list. The source-location deregistration variant first hands the record to Report, which copies it into the engine's statistics collector.

The class itself synchronises nothing. AllocStats holds plain fields — no mutex, no atomic — so its thread-safety is entirely the discipline of whoever holds the record: the manager updates and reads the embedded copies under its statsLock (Engine/Memory/MemoryManager.cpp), and a record copied out by GetAllocatorStat is a snapshot, not a view. What a caller may rely on: the copy you hold does not change; what a caller may not rely on: that the live record cannot change underneath the very act of copying it outside that lock.

Template parameters

None. AllocStats is final and not a template; every allocator, whatever its design, is described through the same field set.
