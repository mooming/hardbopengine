Function description

Defined in Engine/Core/StreamDrainPolicy.cpp as StreamDrainPolicy::GetPriorityShare(); the body is the mirror of GetFifoShare. 

if (allowance.count() <= 0.0)
{
    return std::chrono::duration<double>{};
}

return allowance * (static_cast<double>(priorityWeight) / static_cast<double>(fifoWeight + priorityWeight));

From Engine/Core/StreamDrainPolicy.cpp, StreamDrainPolicy::GetPriorityShare()

Also derived on every call, and used twice by ChooseLane: once to ask whether this lane still has room, and once to work out which of two lanes with room has more room remaining — the tie-break that sends an even race to the FIFO lane.

A share larger than the allowance is impossible; a share larger than what the lane may actually run is not. The share is a fraction of the allowance, but a lane may keep running past it while the stream total stays inside budget — that is the borrowing the class is built for. So this figure is the boundary of guaranteed service, not a ceiling on what the lane will be given.
Read alongside GetPriorityWeight, which is not the same thing. The weight is what was configured; the share is what it normalises to under the current allowance. Restoring a borrowed stream means restoring the weight — a share cannot be fed back into ConfigureRate.
