BestOf

member of hbe::PerfBenchmark Engine/Test/PerfHarness.h the fastest round, which is the figure a report leads with

Signature

[[nodiscard]] std::size_t BestOf(const PerfArm& inArm) const noexcept

Function description

Private. The smallest figure among the arm’s rounds, in tenths of a nanosecond per item. The fastest round is the statistic a throughput comparison wants: every slower round was slowed by something outside the queue — another process, a page fault, the scheduler — and a design comparison that averages those in measures the machine rather than the queue. It is also the statistic easiest to flatter, which is exactly why a claim needs the middle round too, in MedianOf.

An arm with no rounds answers zero, and GradeOf reads a zero winner as REJECTED rather than dividing by it.

Parameters

NameWhat the caller must supply
inArmThe arm to read. Read-only.

Return value

Fastest round in tenths of a nanosecond per item, or zero.
