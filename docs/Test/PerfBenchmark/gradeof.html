GradeOf

member of hbe::PerfBenchmark Engine/Test/PerfHarness.h the whole grading rule, in one function

Signature

[[nodiscard]] EPerfGrade GradeOf(const PerfArm& inWinner, const PerfArm& inLoser) const noexcept

Function description

Private, and the only place a colour is chosen. In order, the first answer that applies wins:

OrderConditionGrade
1The winning arm has violations, or its fastest round is zero.REJECTED
2The loser’s fastest-over-winner’s-fastest ratio is below the margin.RED
3The ratio, discounted by the noise floor, falls below the margin: the claim fits inside the machine.NOISE
4The middle-round ratio also clears the margin.GREEN
5Only the fastest round cleared it.AMBER

The uncertainty term is the control arm’s spread when one was declared, and the wider of the control and the winner’s own spread otherwise. The winner’s own spread is deliberately not used to veto a claim when a control exists: the winner’s slowest round is usually its cold round, and letting one arm’s cold start reject its own claim was measured to turn a 3.85x GREEN into a NOISE that meant nothing. The spread is still printed, because a reader should see it either way.

Nothing here reports an error or a warning. RED and NOISE are answers about a machine, not defects in a build, and a suite that fails on them is a suite whose colour stops meaning anything. The grading section carries the margins this compares against and where they came from.

Parameters

NameWhat the caller must supply
inWinnerThe arm the claim says is faster. Read-only.
inLoserThe arm the claim is made against. Read-only.

Return value

One of EPerfGrade.
