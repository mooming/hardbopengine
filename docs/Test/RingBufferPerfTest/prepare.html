Prepare

member of hbe::RingBufferPerfTest Engine/Test/RingBufferPerf.h register this collection's performance cells

Signature

void Prepare() override

Function description

Registers three cells. The first two carry the MPSC claim — the atomic ring at least 2.00× the mutex ring at one producer, and again at two — each with a same-code control arm, so the grade is checked against a measured noise floor rather than against optimism. The third prints the MPMC cell at two producers and two consumers with no claim on it: the claim this shape used to carry is what the registered cells now measure the other way round, and the disagreement is written down in why the two measurements disagree instead of being resolved by which number arrived last.

Each cell runs five rotated rounds and prints its wall time; the three together cost about 0.75 s. In an unoptimised build the claim line reads claim numbers only, because the same cells measured spreads up to 66% in Dev where they measured 4% in Release — see the noise floor.

Parameters

NameWhat the caller must supply
noneCalled by the framework once before the collection’s testlets run.

Return value

None. Registration only: nothing is measured here, and no measurement is possible until the testlets run.

Example

add("MPSC Atomic Ring Beats Mutex Ring While Saturated", MpscP1, ECellKind::Mpsc, true);
add("MPMC Mutex Ring Against Atomic Ring At Two Producers", MpmcP2, ECellKind::Mpmc, false);
