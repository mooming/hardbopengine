Prepare

member of hbe::RingBufferPerfBenchmarkTest Engine/Test/RingBufferPerf.h register this collection's performance cells

Signature

void Prepare() override

Function description

Registers twenty-two cells, one testlet per shape: the saturated MPSC curve at one, two, four, eight and sixteen producers (four of them carrying the fetch_add arm), the saturated MPMC curve at the same producer counts with two consumers, both sparse curves at capacity 64 with a 2 µs arrival gap, and three spin-cap cells comparing the default of 8 with yielding immediately.

One shape per testlet, rather than one testlet per regime. That is a time decision and a legibility decision at once: grouping five producer counts into one testlet made a single 6 s testlet whose report was a wall of tables, and a regression in the middle of the curve was invisible in it. Grouping by regime also made the worst cell of the sweep exceed the five-second limit any testlet is held to. Now each cell prints its own header, grade and wall time, and a shape that changed names itself.

Item counts scale with the producer count, so that every saturated cell moves about 200,000 items and every sparse cell 10,000 per producer. A cell at sixteen producers is therefore not longer than a cell at one, which is what keeps the sweep inside its budget without shortening the runs that need length most.

Parameters

NameWhat the caller must supply
noneCalled by the framework once before the collection’s testlets run.

Return value

None. Registration only.

Example

add("Saturated MPSC Sixteen Producers", MpscP16, ECellKind::Mpsc, true, true);
add("Sparse MPMC Eight Producers", SparseMpmcP8, ECellKind::Mpmc, false, false);
