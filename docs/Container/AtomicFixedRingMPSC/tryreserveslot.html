TryReserveSlot

member of hbe::AtomicFixedRingMPSC Engine/Container/AtomicFixedRingMPSC.h producer claim: test the ring, then commit the index, never the other way round

Signature

[[nodiscard]] bool TryReserveSlot(size_t& outIndex) noexcept

Signature text is checked against the header by docs_pass.py signatures.

Function description

Private, and the producer’s half of the design. It loads the write frontier, asks the slot at that index whether it is free for this lap, and only then attempts the compare-and-swap that commits the index. It returns false in two distinct cases and the loop tells them apart: a slot whose readyIndex lies behind the frontier means the ring is genuinely full, while a readyIndex ahead of it means another producer moved the frontier and the local copy is stale, so the position is reloaded and the attempt is made again.

The order of test and commit is what makes a refusal leave nothing behind. Claim first and discover fullness afterwards, and the index is burned: nothing was stored, yet the frontier advanced, so no future index can satisfy that slot’s readiness test again and the slot dies. A bounded ring cannot undo a reservation, which is why this is a CAS loop rather than a fetch_add — the measured refutation of the alternative delivered 706,741 pops for 200,000 pushes and detected none of them as wrong.

The loop is identical in shape to the MPMC queue’s and identical in purpose: even in a single-consumer queue, the producer side is where the contention lives. It yields after MaxSpinAttempts failures, and the sweep says that constant wants to be smaller than its default.

Parameters

NameWhat the caller must supply
outIndexReceives the absolute index on success. On failure it is untouched and must not be used.

Return value

ValueMeaning
trueThe caller owns that slot and must publish it with MarkDataReady; leaving it unpublished strands nothing, it simply stays invisible.
falseFull, or the frontier moved and the retry limit was reached. Nothing was reserved, so retrying is harmless.

Private helper, called by Push; the retry count it bounds is the class’s MaxSpinAttempts.
