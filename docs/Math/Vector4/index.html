Vector4

Engine/Math/Vector4.h a four-component vector: the shared vector arithmetic, w-defaulting constructors, and reinterpret views of its leading components

Class description

Vector4 is the fixed-size four-component vector: x, y, z, w in an anonymous union with a raw array a[4]. It shares Engine/Math/VectorCommonImpl.inl with Vector2 and Vector3, and adds what the homogeneous fourth component is for: constructors from a Vector2 or a Vector3 with w defaulting to 1, assignment from either, and four non-explicit conversion operators that reinterpret a Vector4 as a reference to its first two or three components. The w default of 1 is the point-like convention — positions, not directions.

The named axes carry a surprise a caller cannot see from the type: X, Y, Z and W pass all four components (w = 0), but Right, Up and Forward pass only three, so the constructor default gives them w = 1. They are points by construction, not directions, and they follow the same handedness branches as Vector3's.
