Function description

Defined in Engine/Math/UniformTransform.h as UniformTransform::UniformTransform(). Four constructors. The default form sets scale(1) and default-constructs rotation and translation — the identity transform. The nullptr form is explicit and initializes nothing. The parts form takes translation, rotation and scale in that argument order. The Matrix4x4 form is the expensive one: it asserts the matrix is orthogonal (Assert, Debug and Dev), extracts the three columns of the 3-by-3 block, averages their normalized lengths into scale, rebuilds a rotation matrix from the normalized columns, and converts it through Quaternion's matrix constructor.

The scale asserts compare against post-normalization columns. After scale = (c1.Normalize() + c2.Normalize() + c3.Normalize()) * oneThird, the columns are unit length — and the next lines assert IsEqual(scale, c2.Normalize()) and IsEqual(scale, c3.Normalize()), whose arguments are now 1.0f. For any uniform scale other than 1, both asserts fire in Debug and Dev; in Release they are compiled out and the transform comes out with the correct scale.
