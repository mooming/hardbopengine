Function description

Defined in Engine/Math/OBB.h as OBB::OBB(). Four constructors. The default form zero-initializes all three members — a zero-size box at the origin with identity rotation. The nullptr form is explicit and initializes nothing. The parts form takes center, half-extents and rotation as given. The AABB form derives the shape: center((aabb.min + aabb.max) * 0.5f), half((aabb.max - aabb.min) * 0.5f) — the AABB's Center and Half inline — and attaches the given rotation; it does not check that the quaternion is unit.
