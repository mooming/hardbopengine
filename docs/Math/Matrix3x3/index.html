Matrix3x3

Engine/Math/Matrix3x3.h a 3-by-3 row-major matrix: rotation factories and setters, LookAt, inverse, determinant, and the shared matrix arithmetic

Class description

Matrix3x3 is a fixed 3-by-3 row-major matrix: rows[3] of Vector3, aliased with TNumber m[3][3], a flat std::array, and named elements m11…m33. It shares Engine/Math/MatrixCommonImpl.inl with Matrix2x2 and Matrix4x4, and adds rotation factories and setters, a LookAt basis builder, the 3-by-3 inverse and determinant, and conversion to and from Matrix2x2.

Units are mixed and each setter's contract says which: CreateRotation and SetEulerAngles take degrees (they convert through DegreeToRadian), while SetEulerX, SetEulerY and SetEulerZ take radians (they call RotationCos/RotationSin directly). All rotation trig goes through CoordinateOrientation, so handedness is the build's RIGHT_HANDED_COORDINATE decision.

Two defects a caller must know about. Inverse() scales the adjugate by 1/det twice — the returned matrix is the adjugate over det², exact only for determinant-1 (rotation) matrices. And SetEulerZ() writes m33 = 0.0f where the rotation needs c, so its result is not a rotation about z. Both are stated on their pages with the code quoted.
