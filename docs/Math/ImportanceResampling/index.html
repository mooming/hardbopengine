ImportanceResampling

Engine/Math/ImportanceResampling.h importance resampling for Monte Carlo integration: draw and weight, then pick, perturb and average

Class description

ImportanceResampling is importance resampling for Monte Carlo integration, in two stages. Resample draws source samples, weights each by norm(f(x) / p(x)) — the magnitude of the integrand over the source density — and accumulates them with their normalized weights. Integrate then runs the actual estimate: pick a stored sample by its normalized weight through a discrete sampler, perturb it by a uniform sampler, and average f(x) / q(x) over the iterations. The result is an estimate built from more equally weighted samples than raw importance sampling gives — the "Calculate Pi"-style tests in Engine/Math/ImportanceSampling.cpp drive both stages and compare them against a plain MonteCarloIntegrator run.

All three sample stores are public-readable through the Get* members and are ordinary engine HVectors: ClearResampledData empties their contents and Reset releases their buffers by swapping with fresh vectors.

Class properties

Type + NameDefault ValueDescription
TReal totalWeight; private none in the header The accumulated sum of all accepted weights across every Resample call — the denominator of the normalization.
hbe::HVector<TReal> weights; private none in the header One raw weight per accepted sample; appended by Resample, read back through GetWeights.
hbe::HVector<TInput> samples; private none in the header The stored inputs, index-aligned with weights.
hbe::HVector<TReal> normalizedWeights; private none in the header Each weight scaled by numSamples / totalWeight, recomputed over the whole accumulated history at the end of every successful Resample; what Integrate's discrete sampler indexes into.
