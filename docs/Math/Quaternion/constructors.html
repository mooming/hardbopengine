Function description

Defined in Engine/Math/Quaternion.h as Quaternion::Quaternion(). Seven constructors. The default form leaves v zero and s = 1.0f — the identity rotation. The Euler forms take degrees: from a Vector3 of angles or three floats, both delegating to SetEulerAngles. The four-float form copies the components directly. The Vector4 form copies the vector. The matrix form is the expensive one: it asserts the matrix is orthogonal and its determinant is 1 (Assert, Debug and Dev), extracts the rotation by the branch on the trace, and normalizes the result — it is the price of LookAt and every matrix-to-quaternion conversion. The nullptr form initializes nothing.
